QUICK ANSWERS

Good to know.

01 Can I pay on this version?+

Not yet. This version is the redesigned storefront and cart. Square or another payment system can be connected later.

02 How does customization work?+

Choose an eligible product, select “Customize,” enter up to 12 letters, and choose your font, color, and placement. Customization adds $5.

03 Where do I receive my order?+

The concept uses local pickup at Fishers High School. Exact pickup details can be added when payments and order management are connected.

04 Does my bag stay saved?+

Yes. Your bag is saved in your browser so it remains after you refresh or reopen the page on the same device.
